Tens of Thousands of KGs Recovered — Without a Digital Proof Trail
● 6-Month Pilot · No Cost to Project Mumbai

Recyclothon has recovered tonnes of plastic across Mumbai wards through the hard work of societies, colleges, and Rotary clubs. But every kilogram collected is invisible to the sponsors who fund it. Reporting is done on WhatsApp and Excel. Corporate CSR teams can't show their boards what kg they funded, which wards benefited, or how many park benches were installed. There is no live map, no ward-wise summary, and no auditable trail from collection to end-product. Solinexta proposes to build, host, and operate the Recyclothon Command Centre — a digital layer that turns scattered collection data into a live dashboard, a full traceability chain, and auto-generated sponsor-grade CSR reports — entirely at no cost to Project Mumbai for the 6-month pilot.

The Recyclothon Command Centre — How It Works
Solinexta builds a digital layer that turns scattered collection data into a live dashboard, a traceability chain, and auto-generated sponsor reports — connecting every kg from ward collection to recycler to end-product.
Step 01
Ward Collection
Societies, colleges, and Rotary clubs log kg by plastic type via a simple web form or WhatsApp CSV upload. No new hardware required.
Step 02
Recycler Flow
Platform tracks material from pickup to recycler to product. Processed vs. unprocessed tonnage. Reject handling and material quality flags.
Step 03
Live Dashboard
Ward-wise map, partner views, real-time collection totals. Sponsor-specific views go live in months 3–4. Photo upload support for field teams.
Step 04
Sponsor Reports
Auto-generated branded PDF for each corporate sponsor: kg funded, benches installed, CO₂ saved. Ready for annual reports and BRSR filings.
